## Remaining limitations (explicit)

- Aggregate-only repetitions are count-auditable only; nothing trace-level
  (tampering, validity) can ever be verified for them.
- Training-seed variance unmeasured; no causal reward-shape claim possible.
- Eval repetitions share vLLM's default seed; they are not independent draws.
- Frontier/band story: exploratory; all positive CIs include zero and two small
  negative CIs exclude zero without multiplicity correction; partially built
  on aggregate-only data.
- Published runs predate current path confinement, grading/build-file
  protection, and Bubblewrap execution. Their historical limitations remain.
- Some eval-time commits/dates remain bounded rather than exact; model/tokenizer
  revisions and the shared environment are recovered (see provenance).
- ~Half the eval set falls into 3 template families; effective n < 150.
- The compiler-arm "Goodhart" story and the 48-case never-solved taxonomy's
  causes remain untested hypotheses.

Defensible conclusion: the retained dense run showed a small, non-significant
improvement; sparse showed no clear improvement; compiler-aware beat neither;
training-seed variance was not measured; the frontier mechanism is an
exploratory hypothesis. The strongest contribution is the audited end-to-end
RLVR infrastructure, the failure analysis, and the documented verifier
weaknesses.
